Appointment Scheduling and Cancellation Policy

New patients should arrive 20 minutes before their first appointment to complete registration paperwork. Returning patients should arrive 10 minutes early. Appointments can be cancelled or rescheduled up to 24 hours in advance without penalty. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment, or missed appointments without notice, may incur a $25 no-show fee. Patients who miss three appointments without cancelling may be asked to provide a deposit for future bookings. Same-day appointments are available for urgent, non-emergency concerns on a limited basis -- call ahead to check availability.

Emergency Room Policy

The Emergency Department is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and treats patients in order of medical urgency, not arrival time -- this means some patients may be seen before others who arrived earlier. Life-threatening conditions such as chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, stroke symptoms, or loss of consciousness are treated immediately. For non-life-threatening urgent concerns, our Urgent Care clinic next door may result in a shorter wait. Anyone in a medical emergency should call 911 or go directly to the nearest Emergency Room rather than contacting this assistant.

Insurance and Billing Policy

We accept most major insurance providers including Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna, and UnitedHealthcare. Patients without insurance may be eligible for our financial assistance program, which covers a percentage of costs based on household income. Co-pays are due at the time of service. Billing statements are sent within 30 days of a visit, and payment plans are available for balances over $500. For billing questions, patients can call the billing department directly rather than the general front desk line.

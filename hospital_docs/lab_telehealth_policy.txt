Lab Results and Telehealth Policy

Routine lab results are typically available in the patient portal within 3 to 5 business days. Urgent or critical results are communicated directly by phone from your care team, usually within 24 hours. Telehealth video appointments are available for follow-up visits, medication management, and many non-urgent concerns, but are not available for conditions requiring a physical examination, such as new injuries or suspected infections. Telehealth visits require a stable internet connection and can be booked the same way as in-person appointments.

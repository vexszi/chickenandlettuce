Pharmacy and Prescription Refill Policy

Our on-site pharmacy is open Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and closed Sundays. Prescription refill requests should be submitted at least 3 business days before you run out, either through the patient portal or by calling the pharmacy directly. Refills for controlled substances require a follow-up appointment and cannot be authorized over the phone. If your prescribing doctor is unavailable, another provider in the same department may review refill requests, but this can take longer than usual.

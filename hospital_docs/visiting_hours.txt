Visiting Hours Policy

General visiting hours are 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. Patients in the ICU may have visitors from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM only, with a maximum of two visitors at a time. Children under 12 are not permitted in the ICU. Maternity ward visiting hours are 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM, with only one support person allowed to stay overnight. All visitors must check in at the front desk and wear a visitor badge at all times.

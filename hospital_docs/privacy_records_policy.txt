Patient Privacy and Medical Records Policy

All patient information is protected under HIPAA and will not be shared with anyone outside your care team without your written consent, except as required by law. Patients can request a copy of their medical records by submitting a written request to the Health Information Management department; requests are typically fulfilled within 10 business days. There is no charge for the first copy of your records each calendar year. Patients may also grant a family member or caregiver access to their records by completing an authorization form, available at the front desk or through the patient portal.
